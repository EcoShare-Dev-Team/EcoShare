Privacy Policy

Learn what information EcoShare may handle, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 9 October 2026. Confirm this policy against your actual services and practices before publication.

1. Introduction

EcoShare is a community resource-sharing platform. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed, and retained when you use the website and its features.

Only the data practices that actually apply to the live service should be described as active practices. Some information below depends on which features and third-party services are enabled.

2. Information we may handle

Account information

When you register or sign in, the platform may process information such as your email address, authentication identifiers, and account status.

Profile information

Depending on the fields you provide, this may include your display name, gender selection, biography, location text, and profile photo.

Resource and community information

When you create listings or use sharing features, the platform may process resource descriptions, categories, images, availability, borrowing requests, and related communications or notifications.

Technical information

Infrastructure providers may process technical information needed to deliver the service, such as request information, device or browser details, security logs, and diagnostic data. The exact data depends on the services enabled.

Location information

You may enter a location manually or use a location feature if enabled. Browser-based location access requires the relevant device or browser permission. Confirm whether the live application collects precise coordinates before describing its location practices more specifically.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Create and manage accounts and authenticate users.
  • Display profiles and resource listings according to the platform's settings and access rules.
  • Support lending requests, communications, and notifications.
  • Maintain service functionality, diagnose errors, and protect accounts and infrastructure.
  • Respond to user requests, safety reports, and legal obligations.
  • Enforce the Terms & Conditions and address abuse or misuse.

4. Information visible to other users

Some information you submit may be visible to other users, depending on the feature and its access controls. For example, resource listings may display the information needed for others to discover and evaluate an item.

Do not include sensitive personal details in public-facing descriptions. Check the actual visibility settings of your profile and listings before relying on information being private.

5. Service providers and sharing

EcoShare uses Supabase-related services for parts of its application, including authentication, database functionality, or file storage. Depending on the deployment, hosting and delivery providers may also process information to operate the website.

Information may be disclosed when necessary to provide the service, protect users, respond to lawful requests, investigate misuse, or comply with applicable law. This policy should be updated to identify the actual providers, relevant processing arrangements, and any additional services enabled in production.

We do not promise that information is never shared; sharing may occur in the circumstances described above and as otherwise permitted or required by law.

6. Storage and security

EcoShare uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. The actual level of protection depends on application configuration, database permissions, storage policies, account security, and the practices of service providers.

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Do not place passwords, secret keys, or unnecessary sensitive information in profile fields or resource descriptions.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

Deleting an account or contacting us does not necessarily remove all copies immediately. Backups, records required by law, and information held by providers may be subject to separate retention periods. Confirm the actual account deletion and backup procedures before promising a specific deletion timeline.

8. Your choices and requests

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or raise an objection or complaint about its processing.

You can also avoid adding optional details to your profile and manage location permission through your browser or device settings. Some information may be necessary to provide account or sharing features.

To make a privacy request, contact EcoShare through the Contact page. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Children's privacy

EcoShare is not intended to be used in a way that violates age-related requirements under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information in circumstances where it should not have been collected, contact us so the situation can be reviewed.

10. Cookies and session technologies

Authentication and other enabled services may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies to maintain sessions and support website functions. The exact technologies depend on the implementation.

See our Cookie Policy for more information. Update that policy if analytics, advertising, or other optional tracking technologies are introduced.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as EcoShare's features, providers, or legal obligations change. The updated date at the top should be revised when changes are published. Where required, users will be notified or consent will be obtained.

12. Contact us

For questions or requests relating to this Privacy Policy, contact us through the EcoShare Contact page.

Before publication, identify the appropriate organization or person responsible for privacy requests and provide a monitored contact method.

For the EcoShare administrator: verify which profile fields are public, which storage buckets are public, whether precise coordinates are stored, how Supabase sessions work, which logs are retained, and how account deletion is handled. Adjust this policy to match those facts.
