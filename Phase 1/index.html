About EcoShare

A Community Built Around Sharing

EcoShare is designed to make resource sharing simple, local, and accessible. Instead of everyone buying the same things, we can share what we already have.

Whether you need a book for a semester, a tool for a weekend project, or want to give an unused item a second life, EcoShare helps connect you with people around you.
