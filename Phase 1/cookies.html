Cookie Policy

Understand how cookies and similar browser technologies may support EcoShare's website and account features.

Last updated: 9 October 2026. This policy must be checked against the technologies actually used by the deployed website.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a website in your browser. Websites may also use similar technologies, including local storage and session storage, to remember information or support application functionality.

2. Why these technologies may be used

Depending on the enabled features and configuration, EcoShare and its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Maintain authentication sessions and support sign-in.
  • Remember necessary security or application state.
  • Keep the website's core functions working reliably.
  • Diagnose errors or understand performance, if analytics or diagnostics tools are enabled.

This list describes possible purposes. It is not a claim that every technology or analytics service listed is currently enabled on EcoShare.

3. Types of technologies

Essential technologies

Some browser storage or session technologies may be necessary for sign-in, security, and features that you request. Blocking them may cause parts of EcoShare to stop working.

Preferences

If preference features are introduced, browser storage may be used to remember choices such as interface settings.

Analytics and third-party technologies

If analytics, embedded content, or other third-party services are enabled, they may use their own cookies or similar technologies. EcoShare should identify those services and explain their purposes before relying on them.

4. Authentication and Supabase

EcoShare uses Supabase-related authentication services. The client configuration and authentication flow determine how session information is stored and maintained in the browser.

Browser storage is not necessarily the same thing as a cookie. To provide an accurate disclosure, the deployed configuration should be checked to establish the actual storage mechanism and any cookies set by the application or its providers.

5. Managing cookies and browser storage

You can generally view or clear cookies and site data through your browser settings. You may also be able to restrict third-party cookies or use private browsing features.

Clearing or blocking storage may sign you out, reset preferences, or prevent some functions from working. Browser controls do not necessarily disable every form of storage or every third-party technology.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when EcoShare adds features, changes authentication or hosting providers, or enables additional technologies. The updated date should be revised when changes are published.

7. Contact us

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, please contact us through the EcoShare Contact page.

For the EcoShare administrator: inspect the deployed site in browser developer tools and document actual cookies, local/session storage, authentication behavior, and any analytics or third-party scripts. Do not describe optional analytics or advertising cookies as active unless they are actually used.
